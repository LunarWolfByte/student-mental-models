# High School Mental Models

An exploratory investigation into how high-school students make decisions about academics, extracurricular activities, time, achievement, capability, and future goals.

## Central question

> What are students actually optimizing for when they design their high-school lives, and what would change if they consciously optimized for capability, meaning, and contribution instead?

This project begins with observations and questions rather than a predetermined conclusion.

I am interested in understanding the mental models students use when deciding:

* what to study
* which activities to pursue
* how much time to devote to them
* when to specialize or broaden
* what they believe successful students are "supposed" to do
* how peers, parents, teachers, schools, and perceived expectations influence those decisions
* how students decide what is worth sacrificing
* what they believe school is ultimately for
* what students believe life after school looks like

The initial observations come from my own experience as a high-school student. These observations are useful for generating questions, but they are not treated as representative evidence.

## Method

The project is currently in the observation and investigation stage.

I will try to distinguish:

**Observation → Interpretation → Hypothesis → Evidence → Synthesis**

Hypotheses are provisional and may be modified or rejected as evidence accumulates.

I am particularly interested in competing explanations and evidence that challenges my initial assumptions.

## Why this exists

I encountered a number of small situations that made me wonder whether students' decisions are driven by the goals they consciously endorse, by social expectations, by inaccurate beliefs about what success requires, by intrinsic curiosity, or by some combination of these. I myself have struggled with questions of what to pursue, where my time goes, and ultimately where it all leads. As such, there will be some bias, but I strive to keep it at a minimum.

I don't yet know the answer.

The purpose of this repository is to find out.
